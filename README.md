# Hi there! I'm the Founder and CEO of SnakeSol Energy ⚡🐍

Welcome to my GitHub profile. I am the founder of a disruptive initiative focused on bridging sustainable energy and blockchain technology through the high-performance $\color{#9945FF}\textsf{\textbf{Solana}}$ network.

### 🚀 What I am Building

I am currently leading the development of **SnakeSol Energy**, a Decentralized Physical Infrastructure Network (DePIN). Our mission is to tokenize the renewable energy sector by deploying:
- ☀️ **Smart Solar Kits** directly integrated with the blockchain.
- 🔋 **EV Charging Stations (Wallbox)** acting as network nodes.
- 🍃 **ESG Carbon Credits** natively minted and issued on-chain.
- 🪙 **Yield Tokenomics** rewarding infrastructure providers with $\color{#14F195}\textsf{\textbf{SNAKESOL}}$ tokens.
